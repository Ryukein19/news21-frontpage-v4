MISSILES RAIN ON U.S. BASES
Iranian attack hits al-Riffa and Awali in Bahrain — home of the U.S. Fifth Fleet — and two tankers are struck off Oman
THE GULF, 21 NOVEMBER — Bahrain (star) and the two tankers hit off Oman.
Middle East · WarCONFIRMED

An Iranian missile-and-drone attack hit U.S. positions at al-Riffa and Awali. Bahrain said an Iranian attack struck a civilian industrial facility, starting a fire, with no injuries; air defences intercepted several attacks, and debris caused material damage.

Iran claims it hit the radars of two U.S. Patriot systems and downed a U.S. drone near Qeshm Island — unconfirmed. Britain's maritime agency reported 2 tankers hit off Oman, one in the stern about 11 nautical miles north of Khasab, the other in the engine room about 17 nautical miles east. No casualties were reported on either ship.

Americas · QuakeOFFICIAL

MAGNITUDE 6.7 QUAKE SHAKES CENTRAL PERU

00:09 UTC, Junín, about 120 km deep; felt in Lima. No casualties, no significant damage; no tsunami. USGS and GFZ agree on 6.7.

Oceania · QuakeOFFICIAL

09:42 UTC, 143 km deep, 103 km north-east of Lospalos; felt in Dili and Darwin. No casualties; no tsunami.

Fire at the Hartha power station north of Basra after a gas-pipeline explosion, about 03:00 UTC. At least 4 killed, 30 injured; the plant shut down.
